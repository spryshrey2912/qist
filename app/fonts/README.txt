Fonts embedded in the app. Name files  <family>-<weight>.<ext>  (e.g. ronzino-700.woff2); the family name is the part before the dash.

Used for: step titles, the real-cost numbers, the gauge, the legend, the payment tiles, the verdict labels and the number boxes.
