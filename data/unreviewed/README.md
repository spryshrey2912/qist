# Unreviewed web offers (not used)

`offers_web.csv` and `staged_all.csv` are offers collected from bank, merchant and BNPL pages. Their labels were
suggested by an AI tool and have **not** been reviewed (see the `review_state` and `label_conflict` columns).
Nothing in the app, the training or the scores uses these files. They are kept as a pool for future labelling.
